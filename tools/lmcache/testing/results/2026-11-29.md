## What this run was for

The suites were extended for one question: does the tier hold up when several engines and several users interleave, and
when a conversation carries pictures or files?  What they cover now, beyond the single-conversation round trip:

* **incremental publish** - a turn writes one head and its new windows only (also across two different questions over
  the same pasted file);
* **the choice between the RAM tier and the store** - the store is read only when it gives a longer prefix, and a turn
  the RAM tier wins reads just the head (one small object, no windows);
* **several engines on one store** - a turn published on one engine read on another, 16 concurrent reads, and three
  users interleaved over two engines;
* **pictures** - two requests with the same text and different screenshots share one entry (the store keeps the first
  object for a key); the engine's own check refuses a state computed for another picture, so nothing is ever served
  wrong, and the case pins that;
* **failure paths** - a lost window, a torn record, a zeroed head, a store that refuses, a store that comes back, a
  conversation rewound by a client edit, a layer-split state (refused), a read the RAM preflight refuses.  All of them
  leave the caller's image exactly as it was.

## Not covered here, and why

The wiring in `engine/src/program/generate.cpp` is not reachable from these suites (it needs a GPU and a model):

1. the `STRATA_LMCACHE_REPLACE_RAM=1` path - publish after the response, plus the RAM copy kept for this instance;
2. the eviction sink that feeds the background writer;
3. the engine's own `stages.empty()` gate that keeps a layer split away from the store;
4. the one defensive branch in `restore()` for a head that belongs to another conversation with the same opening
   tokens - it exists for a store that hands back a key it was not asked for, and cannot be constructed from the client
   side.

They are checked from the deployment's log instead: `published the finished conversation in ... ms`,
`read N tokens back in ... ms`, `the store's state is not longer than the RAM tier's (...)`,
`not published (...): ...`.
